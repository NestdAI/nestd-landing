The story behind Nestd

Finding a home
shouldn’t take over
your whole life.

We are a student and a young professional who have experienced how hard finding a rental home can be. That shared frustration is why we are building Nestd: less searching as a second job, more space for your next step.

01 / NESTD

Two lives. The same frustration.

A student and a young professional: different schedules, the same need for a place of our own. We have experienced how difficult finding a rental can be, and how much attention it takes away from the things you want to do.

That is where the idea for Nestd began. Not another website to refresh every day, but an app that follows the listings for you. Tell it what you need; a relevant alert brings you back when there is something worth a look.

02 / NESTD

We follow the listings. You make the choice.

That is why Nestd turns the search around. Tell us what you need: a city, a realistic budget and enough room. Nestd follows the listings and lets you know when a home fits.

No decisions made for you. You read the listing, consider the conditions and decide whether to respond. The goal isn’t more time inside an app. It’s helping you move forward.

03 / NESTD

A realistic mission.

An app cannot build homes. Nestd cannot fix the housing shortage or promise you a set of keys. What we can do is make the search easier to follow and help you notice relevant listings.

We want your energy to go towards the next step: writing a response, preparing for a viewing and deciding whether a place feels right. Not pressing refresh again.
