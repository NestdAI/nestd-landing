KNOW WHAT YOU ARE CHOOSING

Support for your search.
Not a promise of a home.

A focused search starts with clear expectations. Here is what Nestd does — and what stays in your hands.

What you pay for

Nestd brings rental listings and housing alerts together around your filters. You pay for that service, not for being allocated a home.

  • Listings around your housing needs
  • Rental alerts to explore
  • A route to the original provider

Where you stay in control

You review the home and respond to the provider yourself. They set the conditions and decide who is invited.

  • Check availability yourself
  • Review the provider’s own conditions
  • Send your own response

You do not need to know which home it will be. You do need to decide whether a paid search service is right for you.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few good questions.

Is there a free plan?+

No. Nestd is a paid service. Review the current price, billing period and terms in the app before confirming a subscription.

How quickly will I receive an alert?+

Nestd focuses on helping you discover listings and respond quickly. Timing depends on the source, processing, your connection and settings. There is no guaranteed delivery time.

Why pay for Nestd?+

You pay for bringing rental listings and housing alerts together around your search filters. Your subscription is not a brokerage service, reservation or guarantee of a home. Review the current price and terms in the app before confirming.

How many listings or alerts should I expect?+

There is no fixed number. It depends on new listings and your filters. A narrow search may return little. Only adjust your location, budget or housing preferences if that also fits your situation.

Does Nestd give me access to every rental website?+

No. Your subscription is for Nestd. An original provider may have its own registration requirements or charges. Check these with the provider before responding or paying them.

Which homes will I see in Nestd?+

Your search filters determine which listings are relevant. Set your location, budget and housing needs, then review the details before responding.

Where do I respond to a listing?+

Continue from the listing to the original provider. Read their conditions and submit your response directly with them.

Can Nestd secure a home for me?+

No. Nestd helps you find and review listings. You respond to the original provider, who sets the conditions and selects tenants. A home or viewing is not guaranteed.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?+

For Apple purchases, manage your subscription through your App Store account. Review the price, billing period, renewal and cancellation terms at purchase. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.
