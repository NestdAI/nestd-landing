Good to know

A few answers.

What exactly does Nestd do?+

Nestd follows rental listings and tells you when a home matches your search preferences. View the home in the app and respond through the provider. Nestd does not rent out properties itself.

What does Nestd cost after the free week?+

Your first week is free. After that, Nestd costs €19.99 per month. It is a paid subscription, not a permanently free plan. Check the trial, renewal and cancellation terms in the app before confirming.

What can I search for?+

Set your city, budget, number of rooms and minimum floor area. The homes you receive depend on your preferences and the available listings.

Can I use Nestd on my phone?+

Nestd is available for iPhone in the App Store. Download the app, set your search preferences and choose your subscription.

Am I guaranteed to find a home?+

We cannot promise you a rental. Nestd helps you discover relevant listings and respond to them. Availability and tenant selection are up to the landlord or agent.
